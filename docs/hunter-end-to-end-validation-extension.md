# Hunter End-to-End Validation — Five-Sample Extension

## Status and purpose

```text
extension_status = FROZEN
prior_samples = 5
extension_samples = 5
aggregate_target = 10
```

The initial campaign and its
[`five-sample synthesis`](hunter-end-to-end-validation-campaign-synthesis.md)
produced bounded positive Hunter evidence with material funnel attrition. This
separately declared extension tests whether those observations repeat across
five additional consecutive eligible samples. It does not revise or overwrite
Samples 1--5.

## Exact inherited protocol

Samples 6--10 inherit the frozen
[`Hunter End-to-End Validation Protocol v0.1`](hunter-end-to-end-validation-protocol.md)
without modification. In particular, each sample uses:

- one new unused inclusive seven-calendar-day source-publication window;
- bounded public Web Search under
  [`Hunter Discovery Policy v0.1`](hunter-discovery-policy-v0.1.md);
- at most ten source-backed candidates, no padding, and neutral deterministic
  presentation order;
- one explicit human discovery selection or `NONE`;
- source-backed translation and the unchanged Event Intelligence boundary;
- the exact existing maturity authority and Futu Browser rules;
- exactly one valid-RTH quote batch when a legitimate option surface exists;
- the unchanged Probability-Free Convexity Discrimination contract; and
- one explicit human option-research structure or `NONE`.

Every sample receives exactly one terminal from the existing taxonomy. An
operational failure remains `OPERATIONALLY_INCONCLUSIVE` and cannot be counted
as product-negative evidence.

## Extension discipline

The five added samples are consecutive within the extension and use the next
unused windows in the campaign's existing reverse-chronological sequence. The
extension must not stop early after a preferred outcome, continue beyond ten
until a preferred rate appears, discard negative outcomes, or replace a human-
selected underlying merely because it has no listed option surface.

The following remain unchanged throughout Samples 6--10:

- discovery producer and policy;
- lane definitions and fact-versus-interpretation boundary;
- no option-market feedback during discovery;
- Event Intelligence acceptance and temporal semantics;
- maturity and Browser eligibility;
- quote evidence and discrimination metrics;
- authority disclosures and human checkpoints; and
- all explicit non-goals and six frozen Engine `missing_*` gaps.

No producer, policy, provider, maturity, or metric may be tuned to improve the
observed continuation, acceptance, surface-availability, or option-preference
rates. A demonstrated implementation defect requires a separately bounded
work unit and does not silently alter the campaign.

After Sample 10 reaches one exact terminal outcome, execution stops for an
aggregate ten-sample synthesis before any further expansion or product change.
