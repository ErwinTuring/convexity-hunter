## What the campaign supports

Under five unused seven-day windows, bounded public Web Search plus Hunter
fact-versus-interpretation discipline repeatedly surfaced events the human had
not pre-specified and wanted to investigate. All five selected events were new
to the human, and four supplied a new event-to-underlying connection. The
selected transmission paths covered advertising economics, transaction and
financing execution, mining permits, asset-sale liquidity, and convertible-
financing survival rather than a single repeated event template.

Event Intelligence preserved the intended fail-closed boundary. One selected
AMZN hypothesis stopped because neither an expected impact window nor a
legitimate reassessment authority existed. The other four were accepted only
after source-backed supplemental verification supplied complete semantics and
a natural future observable milestone; those milestones remained research-
governance boundaries and never became predicted impact ends or option-
maturity anchors.

Where a listed option surface existed, Probability-Free Convexity
Discrimination materially reduced human search effort in both observations.
It supported two different legitimate outcomes without event-probability
estimation: all 24 TMQ structures were rejected, while roughly 28 of 32 PLUG
structures were rejected before one deterministic-geometry research preference
was formed. This is bounded evidence that the layer can support both a clear
`NONE` and a positive research preference rather than mechanically forcing a
selection.

## Attrition and unresolved questions

The largest observed post-acceptance attrition was option availability. Futu
returned no eligible expiration or contract for APXT and IPW, so half of the
accepted hypotheses never reached a comparison surface. The protocol correctly
prevented underlying substitution, maturity widening, or manufactured
structures. This is a real Hunter funnel constraint for small or newly listed
issuers; it is not an operational failure and does not by itself authorize
provider expansion or optionability feedback during discovery.

All five human selections came from `EXPLICIT_CATALYST`. The campaign therefore
does not establish repeatable value for `NARRATIVE_BELIEF_SHIFT` or
`SECOND_ORDER_TRANSMISSION` within this specific consecutive-window sample,
even though separate earlier policy exercises produced human-selected examples
from those lanes. It also does not establish how often the two observed option
surfaces, or the one positive option-research preference, would recur.

The campaign did not test exact selection, Direct Entry, Candidate Assembly,
screening, trade economics, realized outcomes, or investment performance. A
research preference means only that one structure warranted additional
investigation under conditional indicative geometry.

## Product conclusion and bounded next step

The initial product question receives a bounded positive answer: Hunter's
interpretation of public information repeatedly generated source-verifiable,
new-to-human research interest, while its semantic gates produced honest
attrition instead of inventing missing evidence. The end-to-end option path is
not yet repeatably available across selected underlyings, and the two usable
surfaces are too few to claim repeatable discrimination value.

The smallest justified next action is a separately declared five-sample
extension using the unchanged protocol, unchanged discovery policy, and the
same no-option-feedback rule. Its purpose would be to test whether discovery
interest, Event Intelligence acceptance, real option-surface availability, and
probability-free human discrimination repeat across five additional unused
windows. The extension must not tune lanes, sources, maturity policy, provider
selection, or geometry to improve the observed rates. It should again stop
after five added samples for a ten-sample synthesis.
