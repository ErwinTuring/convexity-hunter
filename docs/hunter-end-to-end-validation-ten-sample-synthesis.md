## Bounded findings

Discovery repeatedly produced explicit continuation for this human. Eight
selections were explicit catalysts, one second-order and one narrative.
CLPT and IREN extend lane coverage, but one example per lane does not establish
repeatability for those lanes. Novelty and credibility are human reports, not
objective investment relevance.

Fail-closed boundaries produced meaningful attrition: AMZN lacked temporal
authority; APXT, IPW and SUNE had no surface through the tested provider/policy.
SUNE's positive discovery feedback survived that instrumentability loss.
There is no justification to feed optionability back into discovery or silently
substitute another company.

All six usable surfaces received human reports of reduced search effort and
ability to make a research decision without probabilities. Both positive
preferences and clear NONE outcomes occurred. TMQ and HUMA's rejections are
not failed experiments. Premium/reference, 1x/2x symmetry and representative
shock responses supplied a consistent vocabulary for comparison. Exact
fractions, 5x/10x and full nine-point ladders were often secondary audit detail.

## What remains unproven

This is one human, ten retrospective source windows and six conditional
surfaces, with self-reported effort rather than a randomized, timed comparison.
There was no blinded control against raw Browser or a nearest-strike heuristic,
and learning across repeated exercises is a confound. Approximate rejection
counts must not be summed into a measured productivity rate.

All four positive preferences used October 16. Shorter-tenor premium burden
and symmetric geometry may systematically favor shorter, near-reference
structures. Missing maturity alignment is a disclosure, not evidence that
extra time is worthless; the experiment does not resolve this economic tradeoff.
It also does not establish that an explicit explanation excludes a proximity
heuristic. Full bid/ask availability does not establish executability, and CLPT
illustrates how a preference can coexist with a wide indicative spread.

There is no evidence here of mispricing, expected return, realized payoff,
investment performance, formal liquidity, complete deliverables or suitable
holding periods. Long-dated contractual review milestones, including IREN's,
remain coarse governance limits rather than automatic hypothesis validity.

## Recommendation, not BUILD authorization

The evidence supports a bounded positive conclusion about human research
discrimination, with persistent instrumentability attrition. The smallest
next product work is a presentation-only preflight: a compact primary table
of premium/reference, 1x/2x hurdles and representative shock responses, with
complete audit detail available on demand. Freeze preservation of all rows,
neutral order, missing reasons and authority disclosures before any BUILD.
Do not introduce metric sorting, scores, recommendations or default selections.

Do not automatically extend the campaign or unfreeze the six Engine gaps.
Stop here for the human's product decision on that bounded presentation work.
